What deletion means

A confirmed game-account deletion removes or anonymizes applicable CASCADE account and cloud progress, inventory, linked save data and identifiers, except limited records that must be retained for lawful billing, security or dispute obligations. The publisher must explain any retention that applies to your request and respond within the period required by law.

Deleting a local save or uninstalling affects that device; it does not confirm cloud deletion. Deleting a Unity Player Accounts identity does not necessarily delete CASCADE data in other services. Your Apple or Google account and the store’s purchase history are separate. Deletion does not automatically refund purchases.

Before you delete

Deleting progress may be permanent. Consider whether you need a copy and resolve pending purchase problems first. Non-consumable store restoration is governed separately by the store; already consumed coin packs are not reissued.

Connected account release status

Apple/Google sign-in and cloud saves are awaiting publisher configuration. The required in-game deletion path must be implemented and verified before those features are released. The preview does not claim that a deletion has been processed.
