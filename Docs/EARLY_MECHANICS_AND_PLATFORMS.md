# Early mechanics and readable platforms — 6 October 2026

Design note before implementation. Fresh runs use descent version 4; saved versions 1–3 keep their recipes, witnesses and hashes. No source IDs, prices, package versions or Domain transitions change. Gameplay stays atomic and solver proved; wind never moves the canonical row on a real-time timer.

First island: C01 swap / wood / first arrival wind; C02 repair; C03 bomb with a generous fuse; C04 key opening branch stops; C05 one far-swap ribbon. C06 stamp, C07 hourglass plus bomb, C08 one-use bridge, C09 paired swaps, C10 fragile wood. C11–C40 reuse these warm-up lessons and combine with landing wind and contacts. C41+ retains the previously proved recipes. In v4 early second stages include a visible landing gust after a forecast; the director can tighten or ease the final wood from C06, after the five introductory levels; early landing wind avoids closed-link finales. Every changed recipe must pass the same solver and canonical replay before play; no silent fallback.

Platform information is one reserved legend band: illustrated wood counter attached at the left, animated exact-row landing preview above an empty future plank. Current paired-swap demos use its reserved legend space. Future forecasts hide once applied. No landing text beneath the receiver, no shortest-route label. The projected gap between the lowest legend / hanging tools and the goal is reserved even on compact screens. Preview motion explains left/right/mirror/paired changes, and tapping it opens the existing guide.

Wood presentation: visible remaining swaps, a pulse on wear/repair, existing authored cracking states, anticipation bend, a discrete timber snap, whole-span physical splinters, and later fade. Cosmetic PhysX, gusts, previews and sound cannot issue puzzle commands. Effects respect pause, focus, Reduced Motion, quality and mute. A rolling ambient gust may sway the rig and sound, while rule-changing gusts are always announced before the board breaks / the committed landing begins, while the row still has support. Bomb countdowns use a compact, single-line moves label.

Validation: changed early primary/localized definitions and representative unchanged late recipes; v3 checkpoint compatibility; runtime early contacts / warned shift / fracture PhysX; native portrait, compact RU large text and wide. No phone build/export/install or full-suite repetition.

Proof-driven calibration: seven/eight-piece rows keep the single arrival shift and published second-stage contacts. Their existing symbol/word complexity is already a challenge; piling a second rotation, bomb and hourglass into C32 exceeded the bounded solver budget. Small early rows retain those introduced combinations. All four C32 bindings must be re-proved, not accepted as “probably solvable”.
Wide early finales try a single shift before a full reversal: a reversed eight-piece row would need more adjacent exchanges than two wooden floors can hold. Only those wide recipes are re-proved after changing search order; the other early proofs stay valid.
Intro contact calibration: C03 bomb and C07 hourglass plus bomb are placed using the actual plain / hourglass solver witness, including both arrival and landing shifts. Distinct contacts are printed on their own tools. The full canonical solver must actually trigger them on its witness. Painted contact symbols may differ from the receiver at that slot; their picture always states the real condition. Two hanging tools stay near their real contact slots, with a small minimum separation instead of opposite-end docking. The counter plaque is bolted against the plank underside, rather than floating among hanging tools.
