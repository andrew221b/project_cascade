# Atmosphere and score — 2026-10-06

Scope: presentation and audio only. No puzzle transitions, prices, packages, store IDs or save schemas change. No phone build or installation.

Extend `ToyAudioLibrary` with optional menu/world scores and natural wind. Keep the existing music field as a fallback and preserve original audio files and their metadata. Eight world entries follow the existing `JourneyStory.Zone` order. Two reusable music sources crossfade on region/screen changes; muting and suspension preserve playback position. The wind has its own loop, with gain following the actual shared presentation pressure and bounded gusts; it has no independent audio swell clock. Region changes retain its position.

Write original arrangements sharing CASCADE's established melodic family: soft acoustic plucks, woodwind, piano and strings, Japanese fantasy village character, contrasting A/B phrases. Final Fantasy XVI is a mood reference only; no extracted recording or copied tune. Runway music generation is blocked by the connected free plan, so use locally rendered original scores; do not buy a subscription. Natural wind generation is available separately.

Background flight uses a continuous curved route, perspective scale and a banked turnaround. Far islands have separate slow, offset motion; sky, clouds and foreground architecture remain stable. Motion remains decorative and obeys pause/reduced motion.

Validation: native Editor movement captures, music selection and crossfade/pause checks, loop continuity and clipping checks. Physical-device listening/performance and human enjoyment are not established by these checks.
