# Journey / direct-play surface

Mode: Experience. User explicitly delegates design and requests direct dragging,
an isometric journey, sympathetic hero, simple copy and layered living scenery.
Code-led extension of the established Sky Wind Workshop, no approved raster comp.
Detailed product and rule decisions: JOURNEY_REDESIGN.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A small keeper carries light home. The hand touches the puzzle directly;
the screen gives space to its pieces and a destination, without a control panel.

OWN-WORLD: Mint enamel platforms, warm brass, ivory letter tiles, bright distinct
symbol colors and Balsamiq lettering. Mika's saffron scarf belongs to this world.

STORY: A storm separated the islands. Help one neighbour, mend one route, see the
next island. One sentence explains each task; a short dialogue adds its person.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The active row occupies the widest central space. Top holds a
destination and a single pause control. All floors and the gold target are
visible; three bottom actions give recovery, help and descent. Pressed figures
lift with their glyphs, track the finger, then land together. The map fills its
screen with an isometric illustrated route, current stop and one travel action.

FORM: User-pinned direct manipulation and isometric journey, extending the
existing world. No open identity tournament or seed: composition follows the
explicit brief; design choice delegated by the user. Native Unity/uGUI build.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Current acceptance is limited to actual implemented surfaces and tested actions.
The prior P14 button layout is rejected by the user's evidence; do not use its
earlier approval to certify this revision. Online remains design-only.
