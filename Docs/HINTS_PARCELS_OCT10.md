Design note (authorized by the player's request): hint information is limited per stage, not per floor, and never executes a move. Opening level: three unique states; subsequent stages: one, or two for rows with five or more symbols / three or more floors. Looking again at an already paid/revealed state is free. Rewarded hint cadence was updated on 10 October: the first new hint is free; new hints alternate free and rewarded while free allowance remains; after it is spent, each further new hint requires a completed rewarded video. An already earned hint token satisfies the next rewarded slot. No ad starts automatically; canceled/unavailable video grants nothing and preserves the free allowance. A tutorial demonstration stays isolated from inventory and gameplay. Retry starts a new attempt. Missing new save fields retain the previously granted state.
