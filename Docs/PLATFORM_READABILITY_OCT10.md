# Platform effects — 10 October 2026

Presentation only. No puzzle rules, damage, prices, packages, IDs or schemas changed.

- Fragile exchanges have an irregular full-depth fissure, dark separated grain and exposed splinters at the exact affected neighbour gap. Avoid the floating lightning-symbol appearance. The strength plaque avoids central mechanism marks.
- Closed passages retain the illustrated timber branch, enlarged for mobile readability, seated in a riveted brass socket with a keyhole. The socket shows an opened mark after the real key event. One-use passages retain their physical hinged timber stop and brass foot.
- Linked neighbours use matching spoked brass pulleys and a braided hemp belt; the same drawing appears in their lesson. No purple brackets.
- Mirror timber has opposing engraved arrows; roller timber has directional arrows with a return stroke. Gust timber has static wind-grain markings, including combined entry/gust boards. These are surface identification, not an ambient animation claiming a gameplay event.
- All markings follow the actual bent timber and fade with its break progress. A view-identity guard prevents Canvas rebuilds from looking up old floor meshes while a different level mounts. A count-only guard would still read the wrong board when the two levels have the same floor count.
- The native review uses actual commands and Undo, isolated temporary saves, pre-move lessons and narrow-screen captures. It now rejects Unity errors instead of reporting success after rendering exceptions. Earlier fixture/guard diagnostics are preserved in Reports/PlatformReadability-Oct10.
