# Continuous menu floors and card alignment — 14 October

The marked inventory floor was assembled from a cropped platform plus a separate strip of the original floor. The strip repeated its last rows below the reference layout and used different perspective and lighting. Pause had the same separate extension. Inventory, trophies and Pause now draw one continuous surface from the floor line to the physical viewport bottom. The texture is cropped from its top at uniform scale, without repeated rows. Controls retain their reference coordinates. Frame posts and corner details draw above the floor.

Menu timber cutouts contained pale sky remnants and side leaves clipped against straight rectangular boundaries. The frame shader removes these using the original colour before world tinting, and reuses three small complete atlas sprigs along each side instead of those broken leaves. This cleanup is limited to menu cutout edges; gameplay and map authored flowers are preserved. Generated thematic accents now composite their own alpha, rather than being cut against the old green leaf silhouettes. Accent opacity still follows the graphic/CanvasGroup opacity.

Inventory card title/count boxes share a horizontal centre. The four icons are smaller and vertically centred in their cards. Item catalogue, trophy strip, help and parcel icons are also smaller and centred. Inventory, items, trophies, Pause and help titles now centre on their boards. Catalogue stock marker/count baselines are aligned. These changes do not alter economy, inventory consumption, purchases, game rules or saves.
