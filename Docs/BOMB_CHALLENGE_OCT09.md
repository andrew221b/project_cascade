# Bomb deadline and meaningful risk — 9 October 2026

Earlier durability calibration proved a safe witness, but did not exclude a winning route that exploded or abandoned a bomb. It could also retain the same bomb timer and platform durability, making the blast arrive when the wood would already break.

Fresh campaign and resident-mission recipes must leave a real bomb window: initial durability strictly exceeds the fuse, and the bomb is armed on the initial board. Ordinary bombs receive a fuse bounded by their platform budget; candidate calibration preserves this window. All winning routes must defuse every bomb: exhaustively search the production resolver for a delivered row with any explosion or abandonment. A solved counterexample or an Unknown result rejects the recipe. This includes recovery on lower boards, fragile costs, landing transforms, wind, repairs, keys and hourglass timing. Normal bombs on later boards need at least two useful exchanges. The existing winning certificate and individual benefit-removal proofs remain required.

The solver adds a read-only outcome query over the same BFS and authoritative transition function. Gameplay commands, contact order, early descent, damage, scoring, schemas and stable IDs do not change. A blast is not made an automatic failure; the generator excludes layouts where it can be ignored. Optional stamps remain optional. Pinned active attempts retain their original recipe and replay hashes; do not rewrite a running puzzle or erase progress. New generation uses this policy.

Proofs establish feasibility and absence of the specified bypasses, not human enjoyment or every imaginable layout. Native play review remains separate. No iPhone build until requested.
