# Platform effects and result clarity — 7 October 2026

Entry transforms are applied by the existing resolver when a floor is entered,
including floor zero during initialisation. A lesson must not promise another
transform on the receiver. Discover only future entry floors; explain a stage
arrival before descending to that stage. No rule, ID, save or schema changes.

Mirror, roller and scheduled-gust platforms receive one authored atlas with four
damage states each. The same designs appear in the animated lessons. The original
wood geometry, contact loads, rope suspension and solver transitions are retained.
No floating text or small direction arrows are used to identify these platforms.
Restore the existing pale inset for durability, fitted inside the plank.

The fail illustration separates Mika from the ribbon. Retry uses the existing
enamel control; result controls have explicit equal heights and opaque disabled
states. Validation will use isolated Editor state, not phone builds.

Validation completed in two bounded native Editor passes: six NUnit test methods
invoked through reflection and 48 runtime checks passed. Actual drops were checked
for mirror reversal and roller rotation; a live gust damaged the board and updated
the inset count. Generated surfaces and animated examples were visually inspected.
EN 390×844 and RU 320×693 with large text and safe insets were checked, including
returning from settings. The final pass corrected portrait/receiver overlap and
fitted ribbon text to the rendered cloth, rather than its full transparent bounds.
Reports/PlatformClarity contains the captures and check log.

The purpose-built Unity MCP provider timed out; native Unity was inspected through
CUA MCP with an isolated profile. The temporary runner was removed afterwards.
The full test suite, device build, installation and performance profiling were not
run. These checks establish the specific transitions and layouts above, not human
enjoyment or the absence of every possible bug.
