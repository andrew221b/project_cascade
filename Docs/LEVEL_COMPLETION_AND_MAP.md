# Complete levels, visible journey progress (6 October 2026)

Historical design note (6 October). Current rating/time and menu surfaces are superseded by FIVE_STAR_RESULTS_AND_ACTIVE_TIME.md; current world/island pacing is in MAP_PACING_AND_CELEBRATION.md.

Design note, before implementation. The author's requested normal campaign loop is now: three receiver stages in one level → full-level stars/score/earned resources → earned parcel, if waiting → map. No automatic next-level jump. Automatic ad breaks keep their existing cadence but move after acknowledgement of results and any gift. One map stop is one complete level, never one stage. Each map page is a story step of four or five complete levels; its exact remaining count is visible. Existing 160 node IDs, region boundaries, prices, first-clear coins (40; completed workshop +100), and parcel cadence (three new lights, twelve parcels maximum) stay unchanged. Replaying an opened node improves records without farming first-clear rewards.

Fresh primary and localized campaign deliveries use a version-3 three-stage plan from C01, including replays. Early plans introduce devices at their published teaching points: no repair before C20, no bomb in the intermediate stage before C41. Version-1/2 checkpoint recipes and legacy single attempts restore exactly. Source definitions are unchanged; v3 hashes identify the new recipes. The same solver must prove all three stages, their multiset and difficulty curve before play. A failed plan is a recoverable preparation error, never a silent one-stage replacement.

Access is the first uncompleted offered node plus explicitly completed stops. Story uses the full campaign; solo Shapes/Words/Mixed use their offered sequence, so a new player can start the first Mixed puzzle. Existing clear receipts are shared. Gate shelf/map buttons and user entry functions, including guide/request shortcuts. Preserve out-of-order clears already earned; do not erase user progress. Bonus and stamp challenges keep their existing separate access rules.

Optional schema-1 fields: clear receipts gain best stars/score; PlayerProfile gains the last full completion receipt. Descent gains optional replay-derived tool flags to carry stamps/repair/bridge evidence across checkpoints. Zero/null denotes legacy data. Stars: 3 for a clean unhinted route within max(1, minimum/10) extra moves and at most one Undo; 2 within max(3, minimum/3) extra moves without explosion; otherwise 1. Score: 300 per completed stage + max(0,600−40×extra moves) + 100 per star + 100 for a collected stamp. These measure play, not spendable currency. Rating is computed from the aggregate canonical performance, before animation. Completion receipts store actual reward deltas, so reloading or continuing cannot grant again. Results remain available after restart until acknowledged.

Presentation: sequential stars, readable counted score, warm lantern burst and fabric confetti; stage ribbon shows stage 1/3 or 2/3 with one descent action. Parcel unwrap → illustrated open parcel → earned piece → continue to map. Reduced Motion preserves information with a short fade; pause and hidden menus stop decorative motion. No animation or advertising callback owns progress or rewards. New generated art extends the existing honey wood, ivory cloth and teal enamel style.

Validation to record here and TASKS.md: exact campaign proofs, checkpoint/backward save restoration, access/replay/reward idempotency, native staged descent → result → parcel → map on safe-area portrait and wide. No phone build or installation requested.

Validation-driven amendment: C06/C08/C27 restrict exchanges to disconnected or keyed links, so a whole-row rotation cannot be repaired there. Their v3 final checkpoint uses an authored gust that atomically arranges the same persistent multiset into the published starting order, preserving the published receiver, contacts, linked edges and wood. `DescentStage.PublishedStart` is optional/false for all legacy plans and participates in the hash only when true. This exception uses the proven published finale; its move minimum may be lower because gated planning difficulty is not the same as move count. Avoid adding empty floors solely to pad actions. The stage-handoff commits this arrangement before cosmetic wind/travel; no animation changes it.

Route correction: solo deliveries also retain checkpoints when returning to their map. The map follows the selected Shapes/Words/Mixed offered sequence (skipping empty pages) and counts only its offered nodes; Story keeps the full campaign. Optional `PlayerProfile.StoryRoute` defaults to legacy JourneyAttempt when absent. Availability uses per-island flags, so out-of-order clears and modes with gaps are represented honestly.

Final story access: after all normal campaign nodes are complete, the map keeps replay actions and offers the existing beacon ending through its secondary story button. The statistics/gift/map order remains the same.
