# Night world and journey flow — 7 October 2026

Append Star Observatory as world 8, after the Great Beacon. Existing worlds,
C001–C160, concrete attempts and mode-specific progress retain their order and
identity. C161–C184 extend the published campaign with existing puzzle rules.
The beacon resolves the original journey; Mika then helps night travellers by
restoring constellations with Aster, an owl who must learn to ask for help.

Each world has a three-page authored comic, acknowledged through existing
HeardStory receipts 6000–6008. Pre-island conversations use 8000–8084 and
intermittent level conversations use 7000+stop, below the existing validation
limit. Existing completed-island and checkpoint receipts remain valid. No save
schema, prices or core transition rules change. Automatic story is gated by
actual selected/open world and never replaces or restarts an active attempt.

Night retains the readable candy pieces and warm timber/brass UI, with cool
moonlight and warm lamp highlights. The animated sky, floating observatory
islands and stars are presentation only. Comics preserve existing character
appearances and use localization as live text outside the illustration.

Continue restarts a Lost attempt through the existing retry command, preserves
active/checkpoint/final-reward progress, and hides unavailable travel arrows.
Practice and its bonus-entry navigation are removed from the player flow.
