# Loot-box reward mix — 10 October 2026

New parcel receipts use recipe 5: deterministic by parcel sequence, with a rare
cosmetic every twentieth parcel, a shop tool every fifth parcel except those
cosmetic slots, and coins in all remaining slots. Across the 54 parcels in a
540-clear campaign this is 44 coin drops, 8 tools, and 2 cosmetics (about
81.5% / 14.8% / 3.7%). Coin drops grant 12 coins; already-owned tool/cosmetic
duplicates convert to 20 coins. Existing receipts keep their recorded recipe
and reward. Old/new device conflicts prefer a claimed reward; two different
claimed rewards at the same order remain an explicit economy conflict.

Opening keeps the existing durable claim flow and finite celebration. The
parcel has a short anticipation shake, opens with a small pop, then the reward
travels up from inside the box with an eased overshoot, settle, glint, sound and
existing celebration particles. No reward or progression is granted by the
animation itself.
