# Parcel sprite generation specifications

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, using the supplied loot-box image as design reference; body/envelope edits use the corresponding generated master as edit target. Original generated PNGs retained. Project-ready copies live in CascadeUnity/Assets/_Cascade/Content/Resources/CascadeArt/ParcelReferenceOct13. Only transparent padding was cropped; registered box-body canvases retain identical dimensions.

## ClosedParcel

Transparent extraction/recreation of the exact squat tan burlap patched parcel, golden twine bow/cross, red leaf wax seal, mouse-head kraft tag and two green leaves. Reference perspective and vivid warm sunlight; no background/platform/glow/UI.

## OpenedParcel

Same parcel opened with raised lid, warm golden interior, small sealed green-wax envelope inside; loose twine, leaves, seal and tag scattered alongside. Transparent, same perspective and fabric patches.

## UnwrappedParcel

Precise edit of ClosedParcel: remove ropes, bow, leaves, seal and tag; reconstruct only fabric under them, preserve exact camera, patches, lighting and canvas registration.

## ParcelAdornments

Isolated golden braided parcel bow with two loops and trailing ends, red leaf seal, mouse tag to right and green leaves behind; matched warm golden light and clean alpha.

## BonusEnvelope

Extract isolated sealed golden kraft envelope with emerald leaf wax seal from opened parcel, matched three-quarter camera and paper texture; transparent, no surrounding box or text.

## OpenedEnvelope

Same kraft envelope opened, triangular flap raised, broken green wax seal, visible empty warm paper lining; clean transparent silhouette and same perspective.

## StonePlatform

Isolated wide thin oval golden sandstone plinth under parcel, chunky orange-ochre front blocks, creamy stone top and little corner foliage; reference perspective, no box/background/UI.

## SubtitlePaper

Blank low creamy golden parchment rectangle under title, gently irregular rounded brown edges and dark brass pins, no wood/rope/text; transparent.

## AmberAction

Blank reference amber Claim button, luminous yellow enamel, orange-gold rounded wooden trim and tiny green leaf corners; transparent, no text/background.

## RewardCard

Blank square reference reward panel: ivory parchment, thin rounded orange-gold wood frame, delicate luminous inner rim, green foliage upper-left/lower-right, brass bottom pins. No icon/count/text, transparent.

## Backdrop

Reference portrait environment with exact framing of wood posts/top beam, rope joints, green foliage, blue sky/clouds, distant floating island castles and golden sandstone steps; remove UI, parcel/plinth/glow and static floating leaves; keep warm vivid perspective.
