# P12 — visual direction contract

Scope: the gameplay surface, including initial, selected, terminal and compact
input states. Experience mode; the puzzle itself leads.

## Grounded direction

A bright wind-powered toy workshop above a cloud garden. A small suspended
sorting machine delivers colored shapes and letter tiles into a gold-rimmed
receiving cradle. Soft manufactured forms, visible joints, warm satin metal,
coral/mint/plum toy colors and clean hand-lettered labels. The machine is an
original CASCADE construction. ShapeMania reference evidence was inspected in
the read-only ShapeMania project’s Tools/Tripo/Review/map-phone.png and safe-phone.png, and its visual-material
README: warm charcoal, gold, mint accents, round friendly type, substantial props.

## First viewport

The complete machine occupies the central portrait column, capped at 430
reference units on wide displays. Three/four-symbol platforms are shorter than
eight-symbol platforms. A platform is a thin physical support under a row,
never a large full-width rectangular card. The gold receiver stays below every
floor and carries the complete destination. Colorful tokens are the strongest
small objects; the background stays calm behind them. Durability is attached to
its platform. Future devices remain visible. HUD and large bottom controls are
drawn in the same soft mechanical language.

## Signature interaction

One row travels down one machine. Selection lifts the chosen body without
changing its logical slot; short arcs and causal floor reactions arrive in P13.
P12 ambient clouds/rotors are view-only and freeze in Reduced Motion/background.
The target never floats away with scenery.

## Asset and implementation contract

Author six simple beveled 3D silhouettes, one framed letter tile, platform and
receiver meshes, shared URP materials and device markers. Use one orthographic
camera, one key light and simple contact shadows. No Rigidbody/Collider for
puzzle positions. Use an original generated background where painted scenery
is useful. Text is live TMP, never baked into an image. Font sources/licensing
are recorded separately. Source ShapeMania assets are read-only references.

## Verification

Capture 390×844, 320×693 and wide Editor proportions, with Mixed8, a three-floor
device level and both motion settings. Check geometry, target clarity, word
framing, hit areas and contrast. Re-run real input acceptance after replacing
the presentation. Impeccable finish review follows rendered evidence; device
performance and full glyph QA remain P15/P18.

## Deliberate constraints

The author has delegated style selection and explicitly ruled out further
approval stops between phases. The canonical in-game arrangement is pinned,
so a website-style concept tournament and native productivity navigation would
contradict this task. This direction is an implementation decision within that
authorization; it is not a claimed user approval of an unseen image.

## Direction selection record

Impeccable concept seed 7139683d assigned grounded direction 7; the catalog
run succeeded with pool c3b204a1eed6. The seven considered systems were a
cardboard marble run, a mechanical storybook, a jeweler’s sorting tray, a postal
chute, a greenhouse toy, a clockwork music box, and the cloud wind workshop.
The seventh keeps the compact machine clear and fits the delegated bright toy
brief. The alternatives were working hypotheses, not user research findings.

The dealt challengers were judged on audience identification/product clarity:
cephalopod skin display (declined: moving color competes with symbol identity;
kept disciplined local motion), sewing envelope (declined: pattern notation
adds reading load; kept precise alignment), noir poster (declined: dark dramatic
contrast conflicts with the bright toy brief; kept a strong focal hierarchy),
illuminated initial (declined: ornament competes with required glyphs; kept
careful lettering), ANSI BBS (declined: dense text obscures touch interaction;
kept explicit states), and drum machine (competitive on row/control clarity,
weaker audience fit; kept consistent control placement, rejected timed/grid
semantics). No challenger motifs or assets were copied. The author’s delegated
choice and pinned machine brief govern this code-led build; there is no
approved composite mockup to claim fidelity against.
