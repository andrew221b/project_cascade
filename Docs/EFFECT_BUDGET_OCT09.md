# Required effect budgets — 9 October 2026

Proving one winning witness that activates a device is insufficient: another route might bypass its benefit. Require a separate exhaustive production-solver check for every repair, key, hourglass and ribbon in each newly generated stage. Disable one benefit at a time, retaining the exact starting row, receiver, durability, hazards, other contacts and transitions. Only a proved Unsolvable counterfactual counts; Unknown/cancellation never passes. Reject candidates with unnecessary benefits.

Durability is a per-platform exchange budget, not a generic player move limit. Repair adds its configured amount; keys open their specified edge; an hourglass pauses bomb countdown for two exchanges. The constructive route and shortest proof include all costs, entry permutations, bombs, fragile edges and helpful contacts. Optional stamps remain optional collectibles, and structural effects are not additional move allowances.

No domain rules, schemas, prices, packages or stable IDs change. Existing pinned attempts retain their recipe for save compatibility; the strengthened acceptance check applies when generating new campaign and personal mission stages. No iPhone build or installation until requested.

For generated hourglass puzzles with later platforms, a bomb costs two durability units if the freeze is missed. One lost unit previously allowed a bypass using a later board. This is an authored hazard parameter, not a change to bomb timing or the resolver. Every resulting recipe still requires positive and negative solver proofs.
