# Layered wildlife motion — October 8

Birds and insects gain independent painted body, near/far wing and tail layers; Mika and other characters are outside this change. Source atlases are preserved. No package, gameplay, economy, save schema or product ID changes.

One ambient presentation clock drives all poses. Eight authored wing phases interpolate continuously; birds alternate species-specific short flap bursts and gliding, lean into turns before the path changes and let tails follow slightly later. Butterfly paths have irregular lift/rest beats. Dragonflies ease into hovers, then accelerate into darts. Night moths make asymmetrical loops near decorative lantern light. Visits remain intermittent, with near/far depth, bounded visual footprints and no hit targets. Reduced Motion and Effects Off hide moving wildlife as before.

Extend the existing two wildlife views and pools rather than add another scheduler or Animator package. Painted rig metadata fixes torso/wing-root/tail pivots. Runtime poses use cached transforms/property blocks, without spawning or allocating each frame. Routes and pose sampling depend on clock/seed, so a paused or repeated phase cannot drift.

## Follow-up — October 11

Responding to visits that appeared midway through the visible play area and birds/insects that read as straight vertical drops: when a wildlife view becomes visible mid-flight, its current visit is skipped and the next entrance is admitted from offscreen. Edge routes enter and exit beyond the side gutters, make a broad sideways arc with a return beat, then fade only after leaving the frame; high routes start above the viewport and turn laterally. The ambient route seed still varies direction and curvature without frame-to-frame randomness. Visit spacing is modestly shorter (wildlife's side visitors 32→28 seconds; map visitors 40→36 seconds); it does not add simultaneous swarms.
