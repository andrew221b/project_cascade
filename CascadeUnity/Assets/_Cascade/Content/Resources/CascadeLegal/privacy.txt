Effective 2026-10-29

It distinguishes information kept on your device from information used by optional connected features. The availability of sign-in, cloud saves, advertising and purchases depends on the released version and your choices.

Playing without a linked account

Your device stores your campaign progress, current puzzle and move history, level records, inventory, earned rewards, purchased entitlements, language and accessibility settings, tutorial history and reminder preferences. These files are used to resume play and prevent duplicate rewards. They are not uploaded by the local-save feature itself.

Uninstalling the game, clearing its storage or losing the device can remove local progress. A device backup operated by Apple or Google may separately include app data according to your device settings and those providers' terms. A local save is not a promise of a cloud backup. Optional network features can still contact their providers when enabled, even if you have not linked an Apple or Google identity.

Optional sign-in and cloud progress

Players aged 16 or older can choose linked accounts when the feature is enabled in their build. Unity Authentication handles the game identity and links native Game Center or Google Play Games sign-in; older builds may use Unity Player Accounts. Unity Cloud Code handles authenticated account requests and stores connected profiles, progress, inventory, purchase ownership and deletion jobs in Unity Cloud Save Private Custom Data. The publisher does not ask for or receive your Apple or Google password. Players under 16 use local guest play without cloud sign-in or account linking.

Connected account data can include a game player identifier, provider linkage, authentication tokens managed by the SDK, a device/save namespace, your campaign state, records, settings included in the game profile, inventory, rewards, transaction grant records and sync revisions. The profile is processed to back up and reconcile progress, restore eligible entitlements and avoid crediting a reward twice. Connection metadata, including IP address, device/software details and service logs, may be processed by the service providers for delivery and security.

Linking and loading are separate choices when an existing account already has a save. Do not assume that signing in automatically combines every item from two saves. Linked saves may retain transaction and conflict-resolution records so that reconnection does not duplicate rewards. We do not use your sign-in to publish your scores, create a public profile or send promotional email.

Payments, purchase verification and restoration

Apple App Store or Google Play handles payment and billing information. Flying Ladders does not receive your complete payment card details. To fulfill and verify a purchase, the game and its verification service may process the store, product identifier, transaction identifier, signed receipt or purchase token, app/package identifier, purchase status and a pseudonymous account association. Store receipts may contain additional store-supplied transaction details.

Verification and grant records issue the correct entitlement, record renewals or expiry, handle pending purchases and prevent duplicate grants or fraud. Purchase proofs may be encrypted in the verification service. The current catalog offers consumable coin packs and weekly, monthly or yearly subscriptions that remove automatic ads between levels while active. Optional reward videos remain, and coins are not included in a subscription. The older permanent ad-removal purchase is supported for eligible restoration only and is no longer sold. Restore purchases retrieves eligible active subscriptions and legacy entitlements for the same store account; it does not recreate coin packs. A remaining game balance can be recovered separately through a successfully synchronized game account.

Do not email us passwords, full payment card details or authentication tokens. For support, a transaction/order reference and your game player identifier are normally enough to start an investigation. Apple and Google control their own purchase histories and refund processing.

When rewarded advertising is offered, a reward-intent identifier and provider confirmation may be processed to check that the reward was earned and prevent duplicate claims. Connected purchase or reward processing can use a local installation reference even if you play as a guest; guest play does not mean every optional network feature is disabled.

Advertising and your privacy choices

When advertising is enabled, Flying Ladders uses Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform to request ads and collect applicable privacy choices. Depending on the platform, region, consent and SDK configuration, Google and its advertising partners may process IP address, approximate location inferred from it, advertising or other device identifiers, device/software information, ad views and interactions, diagnostics and fraud-prevention signals. An ad partner may use its own technologies and privacy policy.

Where consent is required, the consent flow determines whether ads may be requested. Where applicable, Advertising privacy in the game reopens privacy options. On iOS, tracking permission is governed separately by the operating system when tracking is used; declining it must not be represented as permission to track. Contextual or limited ads can still involve operational processing and are not necessarily data-free.

An active ad-removal subscription suppresses automatic ads between levels for its verified period. An eligible restored legacy ad-removal purchase also suppresses automatic ads. Optional rewarded offers may remain available if the game shows them and you choose to view one. We do not make a reward video a condition of permission to play. Advertising choices may need to be made again on another device. Production ad availability depends on the build and release configuration.

Local reminders, permissions and device controls

Flying Ladders can schedule local reminders on your device. They start disabled; the game asks the operating system for permission only after you turn reminders on in Settings. If permission is denied, reminders stay disabled until you choose otherwise. You can change the game setting or the operating system's notification settings at any time. Reminders are scheduled locally and do not use a publisher-operated remote push service.

Notifications are queued on the device for the next 14 days and refreshed when you return to the game. Some evening messages may include the number of paths cleared in your local save. Notification text may be visible on your lock screen according to your device settings. The schedule and progress count stay on the device; Flying Ladders does not register for a remote push service or send reminder data to a server. Sound, haptics and presentation effects use the corresponding device capabilities. The game does not need access to your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera for its core puzzle play. Optional device-motion presentation is local and does not determine puzzle rules.

Website, cookies and optional measurement

The website is hosted on Vercel. Its account-deletion form submits a short-lived code through our Cloudflare gateway to Unity Cloud Code. Cloudflare forwards verified store and advertising callbacks and protects public endpoints; it is not the game-profile database. Hosting providers can process ordinary request information, such as IP address, requested URL, time, browser information and security logs, to deliver and protect their services. Fonts and artwork on the website are self-hosted.

The website uses browser storage for preferences you choose, such as pausing decorative motion and remembering cookie choices. The interactive puzzle example runs in your browser and does not submit its moves to a server. It does not sign you in to the game.

This marketing site currently has no audience-measurement provider enabled. If optional measurement is introduced, it will be identified in the cookie settings and will require consent where applicable. You can continue browsing without it. Browser preferences remember motion and privacy choices. Store, sign-in and account-service links take you to a separate service with its own data processing; the browser puzzle does not send moves to your game account.

How long information is kept

Local files remain on your device until replaced, deleted, removed with the app or affected by device backup/restore settings. Connected progress is retained while needed to provide the requested account and recovery features, unless you request deletion or a shorter applicable period requires removal. Support information is retained only for the issue and justified follow-up or recordkeeping needs.

Necessary purchase, fraud-prevention or legal records may be retained after deletion to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or prevent duplicate claims. The deletion process keeps limited keyed, nonreversible ownership and deletion fingerprints to stop purchase replay and prevent a deleted profile from being recreated by a delayed request. It does not retain the deleted playable profile for that purpose. Retained records must be limited to the justified purpose and access restricted. Contact us for the retention applicable to your request; different records do not share a single fixed deletion period.

Children and family use

The game is intended for a mixed audience from age 9. Follow the age rating and audience information in your store listing and use parental purchase and device controls where appropriate. A store rating is not consent to collect personal information, and the game is not designated as an Apple Made for Kids app.

At first launch, the game asks “Are you 16 or older?” with Yes and No choices. It does not ask for a birth date. The answer stays on the device and can be reset in Settings; it is not included in the connected profile. Cloud sign-in and advertising do not initialize before an answer. Players under 16 use local guest play without linked accounts or cloud saves. Players aged 16 or older can choose available connected features.

When ads are enabled for a player under 16, the game requests child-directed treatment, limits ad content to Google’s G rating and disables publisher first-party identifiers. Optional ad personalization is not requested for these players. Consent, regional rules and provider settings may further restrict advertising. Limited or contextual ads can still involve operational processing. This age choice does not replace parental consent where it is legally required.

We do not knowingly collect a child’s personal information contrary to applicable law. A parent or guardian can contact support@vickand.com to ask about a child’s data or request deletion. Store purchases remain subject to Apple or Google controls; optional guest purchases can still require store and receipt-verification processing as described above.
