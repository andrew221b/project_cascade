# Production artwork briefs

All ten images were generated with the built-in image-generation tool. These are the final reusable art directions; gameplay was subsequently inserted from genuine native Simulator captures. Existing Mika art is the identity reference. The selected originals remain in this package's working directory.

1. **Icon master:** premium square close-up of Mika, sage overalls, mustard scarf and brass lantern; teal sphere, golden triangle and violet diamond on a wooden beam; floating islands at twilight; warm nostalgic storybook materials; strong silhouette, no words, logos or corner mask, opaque background.
2. **Icon foreground:** use the selected icon master; retain Mika, lantern, shapes and beam, remove the sky; transparent background; preserve the hero design and lighting.
3. **Day poster background:** portrait 9:19.5 floating garden islands, rope bridges, sky and soft clouds, teal/gold puzzle props at edges, Mika and friendly hedgehog near the bottom. Clean central sky for a real phone and top space for a headline. No UI, phone, logos or text.
4. **Night poster background:** portrait cobalt starry sky, golden crescent moon and floating observatory islands; Mika and hedgehog with warm lantern light at bottom-left; generous central clear area and upper headline area. No UI, phone, logos or text.
5. **Sunset poster background:** portrait honey-gold and teal floating gardens, waterfalls, distant islands, gold key, toolbox and frost vial corner details, Mika lower-right and hedgehog lower-left. Clear center and upper sky. No UI, phone, logos or text.
6. **Home family lifestyle:** portrait editorial photograph of mother, father, daughter and son sharing a relaxed puzzle moment at their sunny kitchen table. Both children school age, all four distinct faces visible, warm natural smiles. Large unbranded foreground phone in natural hands, blank mint screen reserved for the real Shapes capture. Soft wood, cream and mint colors; no text, logos or multiplayer claim.
7. **Park family friends lifestyle:** two families of friends, one Black and one East Asian, exactly four parents and four school-age children. Distinct natural faces, relaxed sunny picnic, shared attention around one foreground unbranded phone. Blank screen reserved for real Words capture. Airy upper space, warm natural photography; no text, logos or multiplayer claim.
8. **Play feature:** wide 1024:500 storybook key art; CASCADE in large cream/gold lettering at left, Mika and hedgehog at right, suspended beam with glossy teal sphere/gold triangle/violet diamond, hanging key, lush floating gardens and sunset. Critical content inside central 85%. No devices, badges, reviews, rank, price or extra words.
9. **Apple header:** extra-wide 21:9 cinematic floating gardens and rope bridges; CASCADE left-center, Mika and hedgehog right-center, lantern and four glossy shapes on a suspended wooden beam. Warm romantic bittersweet storybook art; safe central composition, scenic far edges. No devices, badges, events, price or multiplayer claim.
10. **Apple search creative:** landscape 3:2 floating garden islands and honey/teal sky; CASCADE large near upper-left, Mika and hedgehog at right, rope-suspended wooden beam with four colored shapes at left. Preserve full silhouettes and title, no clipping, devices, badges or unsupported claims.

Native game UI is never generated or redrawn. Store typography, phone frames and final dimensions are composed separately.
