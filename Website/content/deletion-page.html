Before you delete

Deletion removes your game profile, cloud progress, inventory and associated game-account data. This action cannot be undone.

It does not delete your Apple, Google or global Unity account, cancel a subscription or refund purchases. Cancel renewal separately in your store settings.

  1. In the game, open Settings → Account → Get deletion code.
  2. Paste the code below within 10 minutes.
  3. Read the confirmation and select Delete my game account.

The code has 32 characters. It expires after 10 minutes. Do not share it.

No access to the game?

Email support@vickand.com with the subject “Flying Ladders account deletion”. Include your sign-in provider and game identifier if available. We will verify ownership before deleting data. Never send passwords or full payment details.
