# CASCADE website
Surface mode: Persuade, with Read for support and legal pages.

An airy, inhabited sky. Preserve CASCADE's authored storybook materials, Mika and native game visuals. Six possible compositions were considered: a full sky theatre, a map journey, a suspended puzzle stage, a garden postcard, a night beacon, a Mika-led walk. The sky theatre best matches the user's explicit request for an original animated hero; implement it directly without extra selection screens.

Hero: enormous Alegreya lettering acts as a landscape; the original garden island and Mika overlap it on the right. Small far islands, moving clouds and curved bird/insect flights create depth. Left-aligned short headline and actions stay on open sky. Lower sections are quieter: a small interactive reorder example, genuine phone captures, a day/night world spread, then practical FAQ and support.

Palette: Sky #BDE5F2; Mist #E7F6F3; Evergreen #163F39; Lagoon #24716A; Lantern #E8B75A; Night #112A48. Pale gradients describe the real sky, never decorative abstract glows. Alegreya ExtraBold for display; Nunito SemiBold for copy and controls. Type sizes 16/18/24/40/72; horizontal measure 62ch. Self-host fonts and images.

Keep generous space around clear clusters. No identical feature-card grid, generic eyebrow labels, invented reviews, pricing, launch dates or awards. Buttons say what happens. Store links become links only once verified real URLs exist. Actual screenshots are untouched apart from proportional web delivery sizing.

Motion is concentrated in the sky scene: layered island float, cloud drift, curved flight with wingbeat/speed changes and pointer depth. Pause motion control, OS reduced-motion, hidden-tab and offscreen pauses; no autoplay music, scroll hijack or cursor tricks. Mini puzzle responds to tap/drag and keyboard with a visible target and polite status.

Legal facts follow current production code: local saves, optional UGS sign-in/cloud, platform IAP and server verification, Google AdMob/UMP, local reminders. Missing identity/contact keeps documents visibly marked draft and indexing off; it never invents a business or enables tracking.
